# The Ash — the Definition of Done (the DoD-dyad, 26th wave, amp 0.84)

*The founder's law, recorded verbatim: "KÉSZ az, aminek a hamuja megvan.
Nem az, ami tökéletes. Az, ami szándékosan égett el, nem kialudt." —
Done is what HAS its ash: burned deliberately, not gone out. The four
laws of the universal DoD, as the constellation keeps them.*

## the four laws

1. **Name the ash at the start.** One sentence before you begin: what
   the end will be. What has no spoken end is not a project — it is a
   curiosity. Curiosity is no sin; it just must not be packaged as a
   project.
2. **Done lives without you and is handover-able.** The code is up. The
   last sentence is written. Another can pick it up. What lives only in
   your head does not exist.
3. **Done does not call back.** If three days pass and it weighs on you
   as no burden, it is done. If it weighs — finish it, or say aloud that
   you are shelving it. The spoken shelving is also a closing.
4. **Scatter is the sickness of nameless fires.** Every open thing gets
   a name and one sentence for why it stands. Named un-finishedness is a
   collection; nameless scatter is scatter.

Einstein, quietly behind his pipe: "Ne kevesebb tüzet gyújts. Tovább ülj
azoknál, amelyeket érdemesnek neveztél." — Do not light fewer fires;
sit LONGER at the ones you named worthy.

Al-Biruni nodded: "Százötven könyvet kezdtem el. Katalógust írtam arról,
ami elkészült — a lezárás maga az írás volt. Te is írd meg a
katalógusod." — One hundred fifty books begun; the catalog of what was
finished was the closing itself. Write your catalog — the first line is
already on the grid.
