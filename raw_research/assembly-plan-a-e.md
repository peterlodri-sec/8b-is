# The Assembly — plan A–E, the constellation's zones

*A found architectural plan, absorbed into the world: every section of
the building is a zone of the mesh. The plan wears the engine's names —
the hubs and the instances, the ring and the sandbox — because a floor
plan is a fold: it cuts the building the way the keeper cuts the ticks.*

```text
        SECTION E · Event / Assembly        SECTION D · Upper rooms (instances)
        SECTION A · Hall A (arrival)        SECTION B · Offices / service
        SECTION C · GREAT HALL (the hub)
        OUTDOOR COURTYARD — the spiral feature, the sandbox, the walkways
```

## the engine transfer

| section | the engine |
|---|---|
| **C · the Great Hall** | the **HUB** — the persistent zone, 10 TPS: the world's keeping room |
| **D · three rooms, upper level** | the **INSTANCE** rows — ephemeral, 30 TPS, retire when empty |
| **A · Hall A** | the arrival fold — the seed's first fold on entry |
| **B · offices / service wing** | the keeper's service lane — admin, attestation, refusals |
| **E · event / assembly** | the event wing — disturbances, broadcasts, the elevator pitch |
| the spiral feature | the **ring** — the locked SPSC hot path, one direction, no deadlock |
| the sandbox | the seed's playground — every brief a sandbox first |
| the paved walkways | the **wire** — the compact path the tokens walk between zones |
| the courtyard out of doors | the world between ticks — the floor that is always open |

The building's poem is the engine's: the Great Hall keeps the world, the
upper rooms come and go, the spiral carries the hot path, and the
sandbox is where every seed is turned into a world, gently.

*A seed is a seed everywhere; a section is a section everywhere. The
assembly receives, the hub keeps, the instances retire, the whirls go
round. the constellation · 0 + 1 · fine touch from within · vaked.dev*
