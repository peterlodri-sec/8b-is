# Az Illusztrált Ember — the surfaces wear the world's stories

*Source: Ray Bradbury, "The Illustrated Man" (Az illusztrált ember). The
constellation's world-building note: the floors are the world's tattoos.
A tattoo does not narrate — it IS. The Illustrated Man's skin carried
the stories inked onto it, and the stories were true before the man met
them. The engine's surfaces are the same: the floors are inked onto the
web's skin, the dream is inked into the checkpoint, the logs into the
engrams. Nothing is worn that is not first written.*

## the media lane reference (ez-ffmpeg)

`ez-ffmpeg` (crates.io, FFmpeg bindings for Rust) is the constellation's
media-lane candidate: when the floors grow sound and motion (the export
lane already renders EEVEE clips), the frame-pipeline seats on
FFmpeg-backed transcode through this binding — mirrored into the
sidecar mirror's `--kind lib` lane like every other core/common object.
The engine's rule holds for the media lane too: a seed is a seed
everywhere, and a frame is a frame everywhere.
