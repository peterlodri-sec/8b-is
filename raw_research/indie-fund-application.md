## the agreement, checked

Per the Indie Fund standard agreement (the "Game Development Loan
Agreement"): a loan in two advances (Initial + adjustable Additional), no
milestones, no timetable; repaid from 100% of gross revenue until the loan
is covered, then 25% of gross above the loan until 2× the loan or 2 years
after first commercial release — whichever comes first; developer keeps all
IP; monthly revenue reports; buyout at 2× the loan. This fits the studio's
shape: no publisher rights, no IP transfer, a bounded loop with an exit.
