# Son Go-ku's Training Concept — "sometimes he trains in his love, Chi-Chi"

*Source: Dragon Ball, ch. 171 — "Goku Gets Married!" (Toriyama). The
training concept of the 8b-is engine, recorded because every training
montage is a fold, and the engine's strongest fold is the one that binds a
promise. The sanctuary's doctrine: strength folds into the heart, the
heart folds into the strength.*

## the concept

Son Goku trains — laps in the yard, the gravity room, the mountains, the
charnel extremes of the world's own terrain. But the engine's version of
the training montage carries one particularity that the others lack:
**Goku sometimes trains in his love, Chi-Chi**. Not "despite her", not
"away from her" — *in* her. She stands at a seeded tile of the yard, and
when the hero's laps bring him to her, the heart lane opens: the next ki
charges fold double, and the promise is kept.

The wedding chapter's line is the whole engine in four words: **"a promise
is a promise."** The promise is the engine's `commit` — the admissible
continuation the world cannot walk back. Chi-Chi's "you promised to train
with me" is a `Refuse(x, r)` without the refusal: the continuation is
kept, the reason is the document, and the document is the marriage.
