# the metric contestation, absorbed — liquid demarchy on the POP ledger

*The Reynolds gloss (Demarchists: real-time participatory democracy via
implants; Conjoiners: collective consciousness) and the Goodhart problem
of recursive technocracy — read against the lane's own primitives. The
danger: whoever defines "success" weights the experts, and the system
becomes algorithmic oligarchy wearing meritocracy's clothes. The cure is
meta-mechanisms that treat the metric as an unstable, auditable
hypothesis.*

## the one line

> Participatory bandwidth is an optimization problem; democratic
> standing is a rights framework. Computation can route judgment; it
> cannot decide whose interests count.

The ledger preserves the decision tree (what was decided, on whose
advice, with which evidence, what was rejected, what happened). That is
exactly the POP deep grammar's final clause: the ledger preserves both
the path taken and the paths refused. If the paths refused are erased,
the system is a telemetry-laden techno-feudalism; if they survive as
records, it stands a chance of remaining a democracy.
