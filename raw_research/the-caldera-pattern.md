# the caldera pattern — persistence by substrate, control by topology

*The caldera reactor (from the memory corpus: *Why Fusion Success Is
Not Just Ignition*): an oceanic thermo-pneumatic biomass platform built
from its inception for PERSISTENCE, not peak output — a thermal clutch
knot lattice routes fluid and heat by the physical mechanics of the
pipes themselves; no main computer micromanages the chaos; the system
is a coupled totality (break the lattice, the reactor ceases to exist
as a functioning system).*

## the three moves

1. **Control lives in the substrate, not a central brain.** The pipes
   yield mechanically; digital commands would drown micromanaging the
   sea state. The intelligence is the topology.
2. **Persist over peak.** The design goal is survival across storms,
   changing sea states, unpredictable feed density — not the best
   throughput on the calmest day.
3. **Coupled totality.** The whole is the unit of function; a broken
   valve is a broken system, visible before it is catastrophic.

## the consequence

Every boundary creates evidence; every retry is harmless; every refusal
survives; every commit is reconstructable; no component certifies
itself. The caldera is the a-vár essay in hardware: don't manage the
chaos — let the structure ride it.
