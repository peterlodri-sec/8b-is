## the engine transfer

| the blueprint | the engine |
|---|---|
| the section A–A | the keeper's fold — a cut through the ticks, admissible |
| the storeys (+7.20 … -2.40) | the world's levels: the layers admit only stacked states |
| the topography (lejtés 1:8) | the seed's gradient — the terrain IS the seed's contour |
| the tornaterem's double height | a tall zone: a hub's scale in an instance's footprint |
| "metszet = az igazság vágása" | the fold cuts, the cut shows, the shown is attested |
