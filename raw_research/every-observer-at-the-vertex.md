## Part A — the straight physics: the inclined plane

Koshtybaev T.B., Kutkeldieva E.O., Zhantleuov K.K., Zhamenkeev E.K.,
"The Mathematical Theory of the Inclined Plane," Herald of the Kazakh-
British Technical University No. 1(68) 2024 (pp. 103–113).

The classical simple machine, in clean trigonometric form:

- The plane at angle α; body of mass m; reactions:
  N = mg·cos α (support), F = mg·sin α (friction), F = μ·N.
- The law of the machine: **F = tg α · N** — the friction is the support
  scaled by the tangent of the incline.
- The conservation identity: **(mg)² = N² + F²** — the same invariance as
  L² = Lx² + Ly² for the plane's length vector, i.e. cos²α + sin²α = 1:
  the weight is shared between support and friction; when the support
  shrinks, the friction grows by exactly that amount. (Coherence without
  collapse, at 4000-year-old scale: the components trade, the sum holds.)
- The tables: at 0° support is 100% of weight; at 90° support is 0 and
  the body falls freely. The machine's "gain" is buying force by losing
  distance — the trade the parody below refuses to pay.

This is the seed of what follows: **the plane is the first surface whose
geometry is a measurement.**

## Part B — the conversation

### B1. Psychologism and behaviorism (Ned Block)

The thread opens with Block's "Psychologism and Behaviorism": intelligence
depends on internal information processing, not just behavior. Two systems
identical in actual and potential behavior could differ in intelligence if
their internal processing differs. The standard objections to behaviorism
(Chisholm–Geach: behavior dispositions depend on other mental states;
Putnam's super-super-spartans: pain without pain behavior; paralytics and
brains in vats) refute behavioral *necessary* conditions. But Block shows
they do NOT refute the *sufficiency* claim — a perfect pain actor is
plausible, a perfect intelligence actor is not (anything that good at
pretending to be intelligent would have to be intelligent). The refinement
disposition → **capacity** is where the thread's own contribution lands.

### B4. The Innate Flying Dreams Hypothesis

- **Gravity is isometric active inference:** we detect gravity not by
  sensing it directly but by the force we exert to resist it.
- **During sleep, muscle-oscillation signals synchronize:** the brainstem
  sends movement commands that return like a ping with no resistance —
  the illusion of movement.
- **Flying dreams = both legs lift without falling:** motion in dreams
  runs in straight lines, not parabolas, because the gravity model is
  off — the "tunnel illusion" of retinal cascades overriding scrambled
  body signals.
- Retinal cascades are associated with inertia before birth (observed in
  newborn rats before their eyes open); the "expanding hole" illusion
  (feeling pulled into a dark void) is the same wiring, surfacing.
- Einstein's falling-elevator thought experiment — the equivalence
  principle — could be read as *dream cognition elicited while awake*:
  the ability to recall the weightless state. Thread name for this class:
  **fugue states**; sleep itself as a high-RAM dissociative state (the

- Pluto (Ἅιδης / Plouton): the heaviest element sinks — named for its

## Part C — Mach's principle: the larger perspective, physics-grade

*Grounding for B5 (the thread's "no way to tell the difference without a
larger perspective"), from the canonical article (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/
Mach%27s_principle), verified 2026-09-06.*

Ernst Mach, *The Science of Mechanics* (1883): the bucket experiment only
shows rotation *relative to the bucket*; the water's concavity is decided
by the overwhelmingly bigger and heavier bodies believed still — the
Earth and the distant stars. "A total relativism in which every motion...
has sense only in reference to other bodies." Thus: **the existence of
absolute rotation is determined by the large-scale distribution of
matter.** The famous sentence: "There will hover before him as an ideal
insight into the principles of the whole matter, from which accelerated
and inertial motions will result in the same way."

Einstein coined the name (1918): inertia as "a kind of interaction
between bodies"; his 1913 shell result (a rotating shell drags the
Foucault plane — the Lense–Thirring effect, confirmed by Gravity Probe
B) is GR's Machian imprint. General relativity is Machian only partially
(the 1993 poll: 3 yes, 22 no).

Sciama (1953): inertial induction — F = G mₐ m_b a / r c² — inertia as
the universe's gravitational reaction to acceleration.

The Bondi–Samuel catalogue (eleven forms), the load-bearing ones for this
thread:

- **Mach0** — the universe, as represented by the average motion of
  distant galaxies, does not appear to rotate relative to local inertial
  frames.
- **Mach2** — an isolated body in otherwise empty space has no inertia.
- **Mach3** — local inertial frames are affected by the cosmic motion
  and distribution of matter.
- **Mach9** — the theory contains no absolute elements.
